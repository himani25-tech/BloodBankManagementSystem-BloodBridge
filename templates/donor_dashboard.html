● Donor Acknowledgment Card

A printable proof-of-identity card for BloodBridge donors — show it at a donation camp or hospital counter so staff can quickly confirm it's really you and match you against your verified donor record.

It carries your unique Donor ID and a verification code that changes with your donation count, so an old printout can't be reused to impersonate a different status.
